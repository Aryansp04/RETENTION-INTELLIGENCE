## Detailed Speaking Script

### [0:00 - 0:35] Introduction & Program Context
**Spoken Words:**
> "Hi everyone, my name is Aryan. I have recently completed my quantitative data analytics internship with **Unified Mentor**, working on the project **Customer Engagement & Product Utilization Analytics for Retention Strategy**.
> 
> Today, I want to take a couple of minutes to reflect on my journey throughout this internship, the invaluable skills I developed, and share my honest feedback on the experience."

---

### [0:35 - 1:30] Key Learnings & Technical Growth
**Spoken Words:**
> "Before starting this internship, I had built machine learning models on clean academic datasets, but this project challenged me to think like a true quantitative banking analyst. 
> 
> Rather than just chasing accuracy scores or applying generic classification algorithms, I was pushed to understand the real-world business economics behind the data:
> - I learned how to formulate domain-specific behavioral metrics—such as the **Engagement Retention Ratio (ERR)** and the **High-Balance Disengagement Rate (HBDR)**.
> - I uncovered structural market phenomena, like the **2-Product Golden Zone** where customer churn drops by 72%, and the alarming reality that dormant high-balance accounts held over €312 Million in vulnerable deposits.
> - On the engineering side, I deepened my expertise in **Python, Scikit-learn, statistical hypothesis testing (Chi-square and Mann-Whitney U), and building interactive executive tools using Streamlit and Plotly**.
> - Most importantly, I learned how to translate statistical findings into tangible business value—such as calculating Net Interest Margin preservation and drafting executive briefings suitable for central banking regulators like the European Central Bank."

---

### [1:30 - 2:15] Mentorship & Program Feedback
**Spoken Words:**
> "What made this internship standout was the quality of mentorship and curriculum structure provided by Unified Mentor. 
> 
> The project guidelines pushed us beyond surface-level exploratory data analysis. The emphasis on step-by-step methodology—from data ingestion and engagement classification, all the way to econometric modeling and interactive deployment—gave me the confidence to handle end-to-end data science lifecycles independently.
> 
> Whenever I encountered complex analytical questions—such as resolving non-linear relationships in multi-product bundling—the guidance helped me look at the problem from an institutional perspective.
> 
> If I had one piece of feedback for future cohorts, it would be to host even more live peer-review sessions or mock boardroom presentations, because defending analytical decisions is just as crucial as writing the code."

---

### [2:15 - 2:45] Conclusion & Future Vision
**Spoken Words:**
> "Overall, this experience has been truly transformative. It has solidified my passion for quantitative financial analytics, fintech, and risk intelligence, and it provided me with a capstone project that I am genuinely proud to showcase in my professional portfolio.
> 
> I want to express my sincere gratitude to the Unified Mentor team, my mentors, and my fellow interns for an incredible journey. Thank you so much for this opportunity!"
